# Pokfu privacy notice

Public version: https://pokfu.netlify.app/

Pokfu is an open-source Moodle companion maintained by Jad Khiami. It connects directly to the
Moodle site configured in the app and stores the account tokens, course data,
events, reminders, and downloaded course files locally on the device so the
app can provide its features.

Pokfu does not include advertising, analytics, or a hosted user database
in this repository. Data sent over the network is sent to the configured
Moodle site or to a link the user explicitly opens. Pokfu does not sell
personal information.

Users can remove locally stored account and course data by signing out and
clearing the app's cache, or by deleting the app. Moodle administrators may
also retain activity according to their own policies.

For privacy questions, contact Jad Khiami through the project repository's
issue tracker. This notice describes the current independent distribution;
it should be updated if the release adds analytics, advertising, payments,
cloud services, or other data processors.
